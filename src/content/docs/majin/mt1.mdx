## Glitches & Tricks

<details>
            <summary>**Overflow Glitch**</summary>
            The overflow glitch is a glitch in Majin Tensei 1 that can infamously cause enemy turns to end almost instantly, among other effects. The overflow glitch is caused due to a text overflow and is, therefore, exclusive to the fan traslated version of Majin Tensei.

            These are the steps required to reproduce it:
            1. Have a full inventory (30 items)
            2. Meet Isis at the bottom of the Ice Caves (Map 48)
            3. Return to the shrine and reach it (Map 40)
            4. Re-enter and sacrifice any soul to obtain the Hinokagutsuchi

            When the Hinokagutsuchi is received, the glitch becomes active every time the equipment menu is opened. Once the game is in a glitched state, any character can only move directly up or down, regardless of environment limitations or walls. Once a turn ends, every enemy unit that isn't adjacent to a player unit will simply end its turn automatically. If an enemy unit is in reach of a player unit, it might attempt to attack, though the behavior can be inconsistent. Once any attack is done, either by an enemy or player unit, the game returns to a restored state.
            
            If the game is saved, the game also returns to a restored state. The combination of flashing the equipment menu and saving allows us to move units normally to cross big streches of the map while still skipping enemy turns.

            Once the game is put into the glitched state at least once through the Overflow Glitch, all demon skills become corrupted and turn into Paralyze Eye, Rampage and Fireball. Unlike in the normal state, these skills can be used every turn without waiting for the moon cycle to loop.

            </details>
