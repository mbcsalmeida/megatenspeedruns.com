## Speedrun History
<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Any% Ending">
<Steps>
1. The first ever available recorded RTA speedrun of the Any% route, which completes the bad ending, was done by sioneus with a time of **3:31:01**.
2. The current world record is held by Fitsi with a time of [**3:16:24**](https://youtu.be/QOD93XmvsRw?si=GrX3CQxsGJLJdoaS)
</Steps>
</TabItem>

<TabItem label="True Ending">
